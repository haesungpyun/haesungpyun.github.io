Research Focus

I study why information available within language models does not always guide their predictions, and how to diagnose and reduce this gap. I am interested in extending this work toward reliable evaluation, uncertainty-aware prediction, and sequential decision-making for adaptive AI systems.

Research Experience

Seoul National University | Ph.D. Researcher

2023 – Present
  • KAPPA (ICML 2026, co-first author). Analyzed LLM failures where ground-truth answer signals are linearly decodable from hidden representations but predictions are incorrect; characterized the gap via residual-stream probes and geometry.
  • Co-developed a parameter-free inference-time intervention to align model predictions with latent knowledge; improved four-choice TruthfulQA accuracy from 56.7% to 73.5% on Llama 3.1 8B and tested generalization to free-form answers.
  • CombiSearch (ACL 2025, first author). Designed combinatorial in-context example selection optimized for downstream dialogue state tracking; achieved 20x training-data efficiency and a 12-point gain in oracle upper-bound performance.
  • PA-Tool (ACL 2026, co-author). Investigated model/tool-schema mismatch and contributed SFT-based validation of a training-free approach that adapts tool schemas to pretrained model knowledge.
